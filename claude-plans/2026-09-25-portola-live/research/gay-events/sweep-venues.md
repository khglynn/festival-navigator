# Gay/queer events — SF venue sweep, Sep 24–28 2026 (Portola + Folsom weekend)

**Status: DONE — first pass complete.** Web research only. Every event below has a source link. Where a page didn't print a time, it says "not posted." Prices are what the ticket page showed at scrape time (2026-09-25/26) and will drift as tiers sell out.

**Window:** Thu Sep 24 – Mon Sep 28, 2026, San Francisco (Oakland checked too — nothing that read as a clear Folsom-weekend draw turned up there; see note at the bottom).
**Scope:** public events anyone can attend or buy/RSVP into, with a page — official Folsom Street Events parties AND independent ones (club nights, circuit parties, bear/leather/kink, day parties, drag, sober/daytime, tea dances). Excludes private house parties with no public page.

**How to read "official Folsom" below:** only the **Folsom Street Fair itself** (Sunday, 11am–6pm) is run by the nonprofit Folsom Street Events. Everything else — including REAL BAD, Up Your Alley's cousins, Magnitude, Aftershock — is produced by independent promoters, bars, or other nonprofits (Real Bad is Grass Roots Gay Rights Foundation, for instance). "Folsom weekend" in nightlife means "the whole ecosystem of parties clustered around the fair," not "official fair programming." I've marked each event's status so you know who's actually running it.

## Sources worth watching

- **[gaywhere.app/folsom](https://gaywhere.app/folsom)** — by far the best single aggregator found: a purpose-built "every Folsom party" page from the GayWhere app, updated nightly, with times/venues/prices/ticket links for the whole weekend. This is the one to bookmark and re-check closer to the date.
- **[bay.lgbt](https://bay.lgbt/)** — a Bay Area-wide queer events calendar (day-by-day grid), good for catching smaller/newer additions the bigger aggregators miss.
- **[thegaycalendar.com/f/folsome-street-fair](https://thegaycalendar.com/f/folsome-street-fair)** — another comprehensive weekend-specific listing page, community-submitted with a "heat" score per event.
- **[circuitpartyinfo.com/folsom-san-francisco](https://www.circuitpartyinfo.com/folsom-san-francisco/)** — best for the marquee circuit parties specifically (Pervert, Real Bad, Aftershock, Hot Tea) with narrative context and a venue map.

- **Instagram accounts**: [@mull.party](https://www.instagram.com/mull.party/), [@realbadsf](https://www.instagram.com/realbadsf/), [@xoxo_sanfrancisco](https://www.instagram.com/xoxo_sanfrancisco/) (Pervert), [@440castro](https://www.instagram.com/440castro/), [@studsf](https://www.instagram.com/studsf/) — most SF gay promoters post their Folsom-weekend flyers here first, before any aggregator picks them up.
