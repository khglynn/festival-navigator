**Folsom Street Fair itself:** Sunday, Sep 27, 2026, 11am–6pm, Folsom St (7th–13th), SoMa, SF.
**Scope:** Public events with a page (buy/RSVP/info) — official Folsom Street Events parties AND independent club nights, circuit parties, bear/leather/kink parties, day parties, drag, sober/daytime events, tea dances. Excludes private house parties with no public page.
**Method:** Web research only (WebSearch, firecrawl). No detail invented — every field cites a source URL. "not posted" where a page prints no time.
