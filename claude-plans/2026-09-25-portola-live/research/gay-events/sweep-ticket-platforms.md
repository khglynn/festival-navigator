- **[Eventbrite's own Folsom Street Events collection](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/ca--san-francisco/folsom-street-events/)** — auto-curated by Eventbrite, refreshes as new events get listed; good for catching last-minute adds.
- **Andy Matic's Substack** ([andymatic.substack.com](https://andymatic.substack.com/p/folsom-street-fair-party-guide-2026)) — independent, opinionated annual Folsom party guide; a Bay Area nightlife voice worth following for editorial judgment on which parties are worth going to, not just which exist.
- **[folsomstreet.org](https://www.folsomstreet.org/)** — the official nonprofit's own site; only carries the fair + its own benefit parties (Magnitude), not the independent scene.
- **KQED's Do List** ([kqed.org/thedolist](https://www.kqed.org/thedolist)) — ran a same-week roundup specifically flagging the queer/Black-queer-specific parties (SXTPS, Juke WRLD) inside the bigger Portola-week party list; a useful editorial filter for "what's actually good and queer" vs. raw listings.
- **Instagram accounts of the promoters directly** — Real Bad (@realbad), XOXO Presents, Bearracuda, Twisted Windows, MÜLL (mull.party) all post ticket-link updates faster than any aggregator.
