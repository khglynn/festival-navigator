# Gay/queer events sweep — Folsom Street Fair + Portola weekend, SF

**Window:** Thu Sep 24 – Mon Sep 28, 2026. San Francisco (no Oakland finds that were
clearly Folsom-weekend draws, so none included). **Method:** web research only
(WebSearch + firecrawl scrape), no invented details, "not posted" where a page
prints no time. Started from Kevin's five named parties (voice-dictated, so
"Mull" was resolved by candidate search), then followed promoter → other events,
DJ → other listings, and ticket-page "you might also like" threads.

**Status: COMPLETE first pass.** Everything below is sourced; links are the
promoter/ticketing page first, aggregator second.

## Part 3 — Threads followed

Per the ask ("expand our search based on what we find for those specific gay
events... find all the gay events that people are going to"), here's what each
seed led to:

1. **Mull → MÜLL as a promoter/collective.** MÜLL runs a recurring series (not
   just Folsom) — e.g. MÜLL Pride (Jun 2026), MÜLL x BULTO "Year 3" — same
   Fawks/Kudeki residency, same "all trash no class" branding. Only the Sep 25
   Folsom edition falls in the window. [RA promoter page](https://ra.co/promoters/120252)
2. **Pervert → XOXO Entertainment + Matinee/Circuit Festival Barcelona.** XOXO
   also throws circuit parties in other cities (LA, etc.) — nothing else of
   theirs lands in this SF window besides Pervert XXL.
3. **Aftershock / Rated X / Nocturnal → Ky Martinez Events, one producer, three
   parties, one venue pair (City Nights + Halcyon).** This is effectively a
   single promoter's full weekend circuit: Aftershock (Sun 4am) → Rated X (Sun
   night) → Nocturnal (Mon early morning). If your friends like one, the other
   two are the same crowd/producer. [kymartinezevents.com](https://www.kymartinezevents.com/)
4. **Real Bad → its DJs' other Folsom-weekend gigs.** Real Bad's Blacklow,
   Conner Curnick, and DUSTN don't have other confirmed SF Folsom-weekend sets
   this year per available listings — Real Bad is their one stop.
5. **Big Muscle → BigMuscleBears.com** is a global bear/muscle social network;
   this DNA Lounge party is their flagship Folsom-weekend event, no second SF
   date found.
6. **Twisted Windows + Dual Demons → one producing pair, three events across
   the weekend**: Folsom Foreplay (Thu/Fri, Kinkstore), Folsom Saturday
   (SOMArts). Same rope/performance-art production company, different nights
   and venues.
7. **Venue thread — Powerhouse, SF Eagle, and The Stud each run a full
   weekend's worth of small nightly parties** (Powerhouse: Latine Welcome →
   Stank → Black/Brown/Kinky; Eagle: Ashhole → Boot Camp → Daddy Daycare →
   Cell Blok; Stud: Bark Before Dark → Grunt). These are the low-cost/no-cover
   "stuff that's going around" options distinct from the big ticketed circuit
   parties.
8. **"You might also like" on ticket pages (circuitpartyinfo.com's cross-links)**
   surfaced Bearracuda Folsom, Boof x Squish, and Stank as related San
   Francisco listings alongside Pervert XXL / Aftershock / Real Bad — all
   already captured above.
9. **Cat Club (1190 Folsom St) is the free/cheap non-ticketed thread**: three
   different nights (Leisure Fri, Funkytown SF Sat, Folsom Sunday) — general
   SOMA nightlife, not fetish-specific, but steps from every other venue on
   this list and free-to-cheap entry.
10. **Dead end checked and excluded:** "Sanctum" and "Sanctuary" (Ky Martinez /
    Gus Presents properties) came up in searches but only have confirmed dates
    outside this window (Sanctum's last confirmed run was Sep 2024; Sanctuary
    is a June/Pride event) — no 2026 Folsom-weekend instance found, so left out
    rather than guessed at.

## Sources worth watching

- **[andymatic.com/folsom](https://andymatic.com/folsom/)** (mirrored on
  [Substack](https://andymatic.substack.com/p/folsom-street-fair-party-guide-2026)) —
  by far the deepest single source: a locally-written, continuously-updated
  27,000-word guide covering nearly every party above plus DJ bios, venue
  layout tips, and history. This is where most of Part 2 was sourced.
- **[circuitpartyinfo.com/folsom-san-francisco](https://www.circuitpartyinfo.com/folsom-san-francisco/)** —
  clean, current, cross-linked event pages with venue/price/DJ data and a
  "more events in San Francisco" module that's the best "you might also like"
  surface for circuit parties specifically.
- **[folsomstreet.org/fs-events-calendar](https://www.folsomstreet.org/fs-events-calendar)** —
  official Folsom Street org calendar, but caution: it was showing stale
  (2024–2025) entries at scrape time, not yet updated for 2026, so cross-check
  dates elsewhere before trusting it alone.
- **[gaytravel4u.com](https://www.gaytravel4u.com/gay-san-francisco-events/)** and
  **[thegaycalendar.com/f/folsome-street-fair](https://thegaycalendar.com/f/folsome-street-fair)** —
  broad gay-events aggregators, good for a second opinion on time/price.
- **[dothebay.com/folsom-street-fair-san-francisco-parties-events](https://dothebay.com/folsom-street-fair-san-francisco-parties-events)** —
  exists but is JS-rendered; a plain scrape returned only the page shell, so

  good for the underground-techno side of the weekend (MÜLL and its
