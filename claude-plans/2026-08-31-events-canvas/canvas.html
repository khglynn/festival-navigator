AFTERS · A — venue columns (Kevin's picture)

1440px · The main grid's exact grammar, one timetable per night, venues as columns. Thursday is two venues wide and mostly air; Sunday runs seven columns and reads like the real thing.

AFTERS · A — venue columns, phone

390px · Columns hold their 150px floor, so a seven-venue night scrolls sideways under a sticky strip — the same gesture as the Sat/Sun grid, on a night that may only hold one show.

AFTERS · B — night-grouped list (the written proposal)

1440px · Night sub-headers in the day-rule vocabulary, time-sorted inside a night, the venue a map door. Every card is the same size whether a night has one show or twelve.

AFTERS · B — night-grouped list, phone

390px · Two columns, thumb-scroll, nothing sideways. What you lose is "what overlaps what" — the list says when each show starts, never how they stack.

AFTERS · C — venue swim-lanes

1440px · Per night, one row per venue, sets left-to-right in time order. No empty clock to scroll past, and a venue with one show costs one row instead of one column.

AFTERS · C — venue swim-lanes, phone

390px · Below 720 the lane head stacks over its sets and each lane scrolls its own short row — closer to a list than a grid, which is what a one-show venue deserves.

FOLSOM — sorted by time

1440px · The lineup wall's own sort chip and popover, with two options an events section needs. Grouped by night, then chronological: the Street Fair sits at the top of Sunday where it belongs.

FOLSOM — sorted by venue

1440px · The same eight cards, grouped by venue instead. Answers "what is on at SVN West" in one glance and loses the night's shape — which is why time is the honest default.
