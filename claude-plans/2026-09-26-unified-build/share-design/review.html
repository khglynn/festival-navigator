Sharing our picks

You asked whether the plan’s Share is different from a normal share link. It is both: the day written out in plain words for the group chat, and under it the crew link, which opens the app on Our picks the way a shared List opens as a List.

Below are the three things you asked for, drawn by the real app on the real Portola lineup with a made-up crew of nine: the link that opens on Our picks, the text for a group chat, and a quicker way to the crew link from anywhere. Five calls at the bottom have my pick marked. Tap to change any of them; your answers save on this page, and I build what they say.

1 · The link opens on our picksphone 390

With the plan open, “Share our picks” sits at its foot. The link it sends is the crew link with one addition, &plan=open, which travels the way &view=list and the room filters already do. The app reads it once when the link opens and writes it nowhere: not into the crew, not into the address bar. A reload or a later visit opens the wall as usual, and a Back never lands on it. Shares from anywhere else carry the view only; the plan opens only from its own Share.

A friend already in the crew sees the plan open on arrival. A newcomer sees the welcome card first; the plan opens when they close it, so two things never arrive at once. That card still says “the crew’s plan”, a line for the coordinator’s copy pass.

The same on a laptop1280

The panel carries the same foot. A newcomer’s welcome card sits bottom-right, where the corner card would be; when it goes, the panel opens.

2 · The day as textthree formats · plain punctuation

Commas, parentheses and a tilde for a guessed time; no dashes, no arrows, no names of people, only artists, places, times and how many picked. The words come from the plan’s own rows, so the text can never disagree with the app. These are drawings of a chat, labelled as such: the bubble is exactly what the share sheet receives, and the card under it is the link’s preview.

A puts one stop on a line with its “or” under it, like the plan. Sent before the day starts it lists the whole day; once the day is under way it drops what is over and leads with “now till 10:15pm”. B is the same words run together with slashes, 780 characters that are hard to scan. C is short but tells only the next two stops.

3 · A quicker crew linkthree placements

Today the crew link is three taps away: your avatar, + Invite someone, then Share the link on the invite sheet. Each drawing below puts one row or mark in the real menus with their own styles, so it looks the way it would ship. Every one goes straight to the share sheet (or copies, with “Copied ✓”, where there is none) and carries what you are looking at: the rooms you have on and Board or List.

A, a row in the Show menu, is two taps from anywhere: the fest name, then the row. B, a row in the people menu, sits right under + Invite someone, which already leads to the same link. C, a mark in the day rail, is one tap, and it only fits on a laptop: on the phone it pushed THU off the dock.
