Design round, Sat 2026-09-26. Every frame below is the real app code in a headless browser with a made-up crew of nine, where seven picked Despacio (Fay and Gus as a must). No real crew, no database. Branch live/plan-days-design. Reply by number, like "A2: yes" or "C3: bar 1".

  • A. Despacio becomes a room you drop into. The festival file marks it. The plan then only puts someone there when none of their real picks is on. It is never a stop, never an "or", never the NOW. It gets one quiet line a day: "Despacio · drop in till 9:45 PM · 7 picked". Your crew's Saturday goes back to its 12 real stops.
  • B. The open plan scrolls through the festival. It opens on today. Keep scrolling and every later day follows under its own day head. Everything already over folds into one Earlier line. The head and the Share follow the day at the top: "Share Sunday's picks".
  • C. Menus work while the plan is open, and a highlight filters it. Both menus open over the plan and it re-plans as you change them. Highlight people and the plan is made just for them. A stop is where at least two of them are together, and each row says who ("Crane Stage · you + Hal"). One person gets their own day.
