Phone the peekjust above the dock

One row tall, exactly: the grabber, then the tagged row, ending at the dock's top edge. A finger anywhere on it opens the plan; nothing behind it can be tapped through it. A NEXT row's faces wait just under the window, so NOW and NEXT peeks are the same height.

Phone the whole daydrag, tap or flick

Past a third it opens, short of a third it settles back, and a flick goes the way it was flung. The head and the other rows fade in from the edges as it rises. The NOW row's card is grown under it. A row tap grows that stop's card and only the rows below make room; the tapped row stays under your finger unless its card would be cut off at the bottom, and then it rises only as far as it has to. Closing leaves grown cards grown for next time. There's no history entry, so it never costs you a Back.

Phone the edgesclock states

After midnight, Saturday's night stays current while any of its stops is still to come. On a festival's last night, late, it keeps going until the end. The day before a festival the peek shows tomorrow's first stop with its weekday; two days before, nothing. A newcomer's welcome card comes first, and the peek rises once it's gone.
