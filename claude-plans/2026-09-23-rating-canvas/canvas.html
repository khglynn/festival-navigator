One chip per vote

“What about using like cool blended chips if multiple people have the same vote and then those still live in a wrapping row rather than a stack?”Kevin

In the zoom, one chip for each level anyone chose (MUST, then three bars, two, one), loudest first, all in one wrapping row. Each chip is filled with the colours of the people at that level, blended, and says its level with the card meter’s own glyph; the chip you are in wears your white edge. Every card and zoom is the app’s own code, running live: rest the mouse on a card or hold it, then tap the grown card to turn yourself up and watch your letter move between chips. Slow motion is in the bar.

Kevin’s chip, three takes

They differ in what fills a chip and what sits inside it. One person alone at a level is just their colour in all three; the text is the card’s own white.

aura mix · letters

1 · Aura chips

Each chip is filled with the card’s own aura mix of its people, as bright as their level, so a MUST chip glows and a one-bar chip is quiet. The people are a tight run of letters; letters stay readable on any blend, but you have to know whose letter is whose.

smooth blend · avatars

2 · Avatars on a blend

A smooth left-to-right blend, with each person as the app’s own letter avatar overlapping the next, in their colour. The prettiest with two or three people; with five, a chip becomes a row of circles in a pill.

stripes · names

3 · Stripes and names

Hard stripes, one per person, so you can count the chip; up to two first names, then “+n”. It is the only take that names people, but the words sit on stripes, which is the hardest background to read text on.

How this page was made

The app’s own modules and Portola’s real festival file are bundled into the page unchanged, apart from two hooks: one when a card is made (the meter, decided in round 2) and one when the zoom’s lines are built (the chips, so the zoom measures and blooms them like its own). The blends come from aura.js and palette.js, the way the card’s aura does. The crew is made up and lives only in memory; nothing talks to the app’s server or database.
